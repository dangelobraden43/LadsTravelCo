description: Durable source lessons for timing/events research - which pages are empty, which list stale years, how search summaries mislead on dates

  type: reference
---

- **NPS `planyourvisit/visitorcenters.htm` pages can have EMPTY "Hours & Seasons" blocks** (seen at Pictured Rocks, Oct 2026). Do not spend a fetch expecting season dates there; `hours.htm` is also generic. The park's `winter-road-closures.htm` and `insects.htm` pages are the concrete ones.
- **Local visitor-bureau "Annual Events" pages give reliable recurring PATTERNS ("last Sunday in June") but stale-year dates** (munising.org listed 2024 dates in late 2026). Record the pattern; never publish the old year's date or a computed next date without confirming.
- **WebSearch answer summaries state future event dates without naming which page they came from.** Treat as snippet-only, confidence low, and flag for the verifier; cite the event's own listing.
- **Visitation figures in press coverage cluster on record years** (2020-2021 pandemic). NPS IRMA stats are the real source for a current crowd curve; budget one call for it.
- **The trap season pattern for northern parks:** insect season (black flies mid-May to mid-June) and the post-cruise / pre-snowmobile gap (late Oct to Nov) are the two "cheap for a reason" windows. Check both. See [[timing-budget-plan]].
- **Search summaries can get weekdays wrong** (a school-calendar summary called a Tuesday "Monday"). Check every weekday/date pair yourself; if one is wrong, mark all dates from that summary low.
- **OPM `federal-holidays/` page is a one-fetch source for US holidays a year out** (read Oct 2026, had 2027). Use it instead of searching holidays.
- **news.wttw.com returns 403 on fetch.** Snippet only.
- **Ski seasons:** resort-announced opening targets are not out by early October. A regional press roundup of last season's openings (e.g. Northern Express for N. Michigan) gives per-resort dates in one read; resort press releases give late-season traps (one-run "still open"). Lake Michigan car ferries are seasonal and are a winter logistics closure.
- **Eclipse runs:** timeanddate.com city eclipse pages 403 on fetch (all cities). NASA GSFC `SEpath/.../SE<date>Tpath.html` fetches clean (limits, sun alt, duration by UT) and the `SEplot` GIF is readable as an image via Read. IGN astronomia.ign.es eclipse page gives headline towns but its municipality table renders empty. Eclipsophile 2027 is at `eclipsophile.com/?p=4226` (the /2027tse/ slug 404s). See [[event-season-sources]].
- **AEMET climate normals fetch clean** at `aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=<station>&k=<region>` (Sevilla 5783, Malaga 6155A): all 12 months, mean max, rain days, clear days. Use instead of Wikipedia climate tables.
- **Spanish regional holidays:** BOJA PDFs do not render on fetch; press (andaluciainforma.es, which eldiario subdomain 301s to) plus diainternacionalde.com calendar pages read clean.
- **Big-tournament fixtures (RWC 2027 run, Oct 2026):** organiser match pages render client-side and fetch empty; the governing body's news releases (world.rugby/news/...) fetch clean with phase dates; participating national unions' schedule pages (scottishrugby.org) carry venue-local kick-off times; the state stadium operator (stadiums.qld.gov.au) lists its venue's full fixture. Ask the organiser /news index for article URLs instead of searching.
- **Australia gov sources:** state education "future term dates" pages (NSW, WA, QLD `/about-us/calendar/future-dates`, vic.gov.au) and nsw.gov.au / qld.gov.au public-holiday pages fetch clean a year out. bom.gov.au, timeanddate.com, sa.gov.au, education.sa.gov.au, business.vic.gov.au = 403; fairwork.gov.au times out. Climate: australia.com + climatestotravel.com city pages (australia.com's Melbourne row duplicated Sydney's).
- **Ticket phases:** past phases go in one CLOSED-history finding; the open phase gets a re-check datedUntil (today+30) since no end date is published.
- **Sun/moon times without timeanddate:** USNO API fetches clean JSON (`aa.usno.navy.mil/api/rstt/oneday?date=YYYY-MM-DD&coords=lat,lng&tz=-4` for sun+moon rise/set; `/api/moon/phases/date?date=...&nump=4`). sunrise-sunset.org `/json?...&formatted=0` (UTC) is the second source. Always check the moon phase: a full moon kills a "dark sky" stop.
- **NPS park pages vary:** Shenandoah `hours.htm` carries the full concession/campground season table (dates by mile); its `weather.htm`, `seasons.htm`, `visitorcenters.htm` are empty and `conditions.htm` showed a 2024 stale alert block. NPS fall colour reports are video-only. Concessioner sites (goshenandoah.com `/service-updates`, `/activities-events/...`) hold hours and events. recreation.gov `/ticket/facility/<id>` fetches clean with construction notices. nationalparkstraveler.org = 403.
- **Foliage forecast:** morethanjustparks.com/foliage-tracker/<state> gives a dated per-park model peak; label it a model (its own tables can disagree). State tourism foliage maps are graphics with no fetchable dates.
- **College sports dates:** warrennolan.com schedule pages fetch clean; team athletics sites render empty.
- **Planning inputs mislabel weekdays** ("both days are weekdays" when arrival was a Sunday). Compute every trip date's weekday yourself.
- **River levels, zero search cost:** USGS `waterservices.usgs.gov/nwis/iv/?sites=<id>&parameterCd=00060,00065&period=P7D&format=rdb` (live) and `/nwis/stat/?sites=<id>&statReportType=daily&statTypeCd=p10,p50,p90&parameterCd=00060&format=rdb` (daily percentiles) both fetch clean. The `waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/` page shows no values. Live flow can be wildly off-median days before a trip: record it as a dated snapshot trap.
- **NPS closures:** `planyourvisit/road-and-trail-closures.htm` is the concrete page (dated, per-trail day-of-week rules); `conditions.htm` only links. Check weekday rules against the actual trip weekdays rather than copying a founder plan's reading.
- **State parks (WV):** wvstateparks.com park pages carry no seasonal ops (empty "Park Conditions"). Tram/attraction status lives in local TV press (digital-release.wvnstv.com fetches clean).
- **Founder trip-plan inputs assert events that may not exist this year** (a "Fall Festival this Saturday" with no 2026 listing). Check every event in an input file for a current-year listing.
- **Tivoli trap, waterfall edition:** "Sandstone Falls" returns Michigan's Ottawa NF site before WV's. Same name, different park; confirm the park in every result.
- **The session-wide WebSearch cap is shared by every agent** (200/session, Oct 17 2026) and can run out before your own budget does. Spend searches on the highest-value unknowns first; WebFetch still works after the cap, so keep fetch-able official URLs for last. Record refused searches as `could-not-look`.
- **This agent may have no Bash tool**, so `validate.mjs` cannot be run by hand; the SubagentStop hook validates. Self-check for currency amounts, "free", and first-person phrases with Grep before finishing.
- **WebFetch can be refused for the whole run** (permission not granted, Galway run Oct 17 2026). Test one fetch first; if refused, re-plan as search-only: every finding becomes snippet-only, so pick queries that surface official pages in results, and record official-page reads as `could-not-look` gaps. Reuse head-start runs' READ sources (USNO, Met Éireann) by citing them with their original checkedOn.
- **Trip-week refresh sources (Gorge & Ridge run, Oct 21 2026), all fetch clean:** NWS `api.weather.gov/points/<lat>,<lng>` then its `gridpoints/<office>/<x>,<y>/forecast` (7 days, PoP, elevation); the Area Forecast Discussion as text at `forecast.weather.gov/product.php?site=<OFF>&issuedby=<OFF>&product=AFD&format=txt&version=1&glossary=0` (fog/pattern). recreation.gov `/api/ticket/availability/facility/<id>?date=YYYY-MM-DD` returns per-pool inventory and reservation counts (a just-released pool can show 0 reserved: say "feed shows", not "tickets left"). wvdnr.gov/hunting/hunting-seasons/, dwr.virginia.gov/hunting/regulations/deer/, wvforestry.com home (fall fire season), transportation.wv.gov/news (WVDOT releases). **404:** nps.gov `<park>/planyourvisit/alerts.htm`, shen `road-and-trail-closures.htm`, dwr.virginia.gov `/hunting/season-dates/`, wvforestry `/fire-protection/burning-laws/`. NPS alert banners and wv511.org are client-side: record a gap.
- **Trap: moon phase times cross midnight.** A full moon at 04:12 UT is 00:12 EDT the SAME date, the night before the day a plan names. Check plan moon lines against USNO with the local tz.
- Budget note: 12 calls covers ~4 windows + events + closures only if fetches are spent on official pages; school breaks and weather normals got squeezed out. Plan one call each up front.
