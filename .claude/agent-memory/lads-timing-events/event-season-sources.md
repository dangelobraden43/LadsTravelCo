- **Fetch-first tactic works:** WebFetch of official organiser sites does not count against the search budget. Guess the organiser domain and fetch before searching; spend searches only to discover URLs. (Christmas markets run, Oct 2026: 25 findings on 12 searches.)
- **German/Austrian/Swiss/French/Belgian organisers post next season's dates by early October** and fetch cleanly: christkindlesmarkt.de, christkindlmarkt-muenchen.de (city-run), koelnerweihnachtsmarkt.com, christkindlmarkt.at, christkindlmarkt.co.at, bs.ch/weihnachtsmarkt, montreuxnoel.com/en/, plaisirsdhiver.be/en/practical-info, noel-colmar.com/en/.../practical, adventbazilika.hu/en/opening-hours/, tivoli.dk/en/jul.
- **Czech, Polish, Hungarian city markets publish late** (Nov). In early October expect gaps for Prague, Kraków, Wrocław, Gdańsk, Vörösmarty tér; record prior-year pattern in the gap detail, not as a finding. wroclaw.pl news articles carry full per-day exceptions for the prior year.
- **Blocking/broken:** dresden.de 503'd all day; striezelmarkt.dresden.de does not resolve; prague.eu /en/event/ URLs 404; visitstrasbourg.fr 403; oesterreich.gv.at holiday URLs 404; hospitality-on.com 403.
- **noel.strasbourg.eu FAQ carries LAST year's text in early October** while the homepage shows the new opening date. By Nov 10 (2026) `/foire-aux-questions` had the new year's full hours, closing day and bridge access controls: check the year on it, don't assume.
- **Mid-November re-check sources (Nov 2026), fetch clean:** christkindlesmarkt.de German home (EN page omits 24 Dec hours; /en/visitor-info/opening-hours/ 404), bs.ch/weihnachtsmarkt, noel-colmar.com practical page (pedestrian-zone + shuttle dates) and its /en/ home (all Alsace village dates in one fetch), rothenburg-tourismus.de/reiterlesmarkt, polarzauber.ch, bellevuenoel.ch, adventzagreb.hr, prague.eu/cs/akce/... (dates, no hours). **Moved:** plaisirsdhiver.be/en/* now 301s to winterpret.be/en (practical page /en/practical-information). **Still 503:** dresden.de (all pages, two years running); search snippets of its tender PDF/press release are the only route. zuerich.com christmas-markets URL 404.
- **Trap pattern: a security incident at the market square weeks before opening** (Kraków Rynek Główny shooting, 7 Nov 2026). City home pages (krakow.pl) show barriers/mourning items before any market statement. For a re-check, read each city's home page, not just the market page.
- **KMK Ferienkalender PDF is unreadable via fetch** (binary, no pdftoppm). Use state ministry pages: km.bayern.de/ministerium/termine/ferientermine.html works.
- **Wikipedia climate tables via WebFetch return wrong numbers** (record highs read as mean max). Do not use; tourism-board climate pages or climate aggregators read cleaner.
- **Trap pattern: the eclipse-trio year mix-up.** Spain had eclipses on 12 Aug 2026 (passed), 2 Aug 2027 (total, south) and 26 Jan 2028 (annular). Search results, IGN weather pages and price stories blend 2026 and 2027; check the year on every page. The Spanish first-of-August DGT road exodus and the Strait's summer crossing operation (OPE, mid-June to mid-Sept) are recurring Spain summer logistics traps worth one search each.
- **Ireland (Oct 2026 Dublin/Galway fix pass):** giaf.ie, stpatricksfestival.ie and galwayraces.com/summer-festival/ post NEXT year's dates by October and fetch cleanly (racecourse body text lags its headline). transportforireland.ie/christmas-services/ gives every operator's 24-26 Dec pattern in one fetch. thisisgalway.ie wrap-up articles carry venue + attendance. Irish hotel demand by season: STR talk at the IHF conference, reported by travelextra.ie (Feb). citizensinformation.ie 403s; galwaychristmasmarket.com/.ie dead; guinness-storehouse.com FAQ has empty answers.
- **Ireland a year out (Ryder Cup run, Oct 2026):** transportforireland.ie/events/page/N/ is chronological; the LAST pages list next year's big events (concerts, races, Ploughing) in one fetch each, free. Met Éireann station averages fetch clean from the links on met.ie/climate/30-year-averages (Shannon 1991-2020; Valentia only 1981-2010). listowelraces.ie, dublintheatrefestival.ie, matchmakerireland.com show next-year dates; irishexaminer.com, farmersjournal.ie, travelextra.ie, limerickpost.ie, holidaydb.com read clean. **403:** limerickleader.ie, agriland.ie, kildarenow.com, gov.ie OPW campaign pages. rydercup.com schedule page has no times a year out.
- **Dublin a year out (Oct 2026):** avivastadium.ie/events fetches the full calendar clean; crokepark.ie/events and 3arena.ie/events render EMPTY (client-side). TFI events pagination shifts as events pass (page 6 went empty a day later; the calendar end moves). visitdublin.com event pages carry exact festival dates when fringefest.com does not. irishlifedublinmarathon.ie, tcd.ie FAQ, hoganstand.com, rugby.com.au read clean. **403:** museum.ie, australia.rugby, act.gov.au. Met Éireann Dublin Airport normals: met.ie/cms/assets/uploads/2023/09/www_met_ie_dublin_9120.htm.
- **Trap pattern: the national team plays abroad the same day.** Check the home nation's away fixtures (rugby warm-ups, qualifiers) on the event's match days: pubs get double-booked screens (Ireland v Australia in Canberra on Ryder Cup Saturday 2027).
- **Trap pattern: the reform headline that reads as law.** Licensing/opening-hours reforms announced years ago (Ireland's Sale of Alcohol Bill, 2022) still circulate as current. Find a recent press status line before stating hours.
- **Ireland, Limerick (Oct 17 2026 run):** fixture lists hidden in images: WordPress `-1024x637.avif` fetches as unreadable binary, but swapping to the original `<name>.png` saves a file the Read tool can view (limerickraces.ie 2027 fixtures). limerick.ie council news, ul.ie academic calendar, limerickpost.ie `/tag/ryder-cup/` (dated URLs) and cliffsofmoher.ie opening-hours fetch clean. **eva.ie 403.** limerick.ie PDFs are compressed and Read cannot render PDFs here (no pdftoppm): skip them. TFI events calendar is short: the last non-empty page holds the latest events (page 6 went empty). Search summaries describe past one-off events (a countdown concert) with no date: always find the dated article.
- **Trap pattern: mega-event displacement.** Other national events move to dodge a mega-event (Ploughing 2027 shifted a week to the trip's departure days). Check the week AFTER the event, not just the event week.
- **Trap pattern: the folklore event that lives elsewhere.** Guides attach a national custom to the city (Wren Day "parades" in Dublin/Galway; it survives in Dingle/West Kerry). Check WHERE, not just whether, a custom happens.
- **Re-check passes:** fix-pass briefs want per-claim verdicts; venue moves (GIAF Big Top Nimmo's Pier -> Fisheries Field) and "N-day festival" lengths that change with the weekday of a fixed date are the usual CORRECTED items.
- **Galway / Connemara (Oct 17 2026, search-only):** discoverireland.ie and thisisgalway.ie snippets carry exact festival dates (Oyster Fest, Clifden Arts); galwaytourism.ie lists next-year dates but marks them tbc. Two oyster festivals (Galway city, last Sept weekend; Clarenbridge village, early-mid Sept) get blended. **Trap pattern: the expiring state contract.** Island air services run on fixed-term PSO contracts (Aran: 2022-2026); check the successor before writing any next-year flight as running.
- **Christmas-market mid-Nov re-check (Nov 2026), 13 markets on 27 calls:** wien.info/en/shopping-wining-dining/markets/christmas-markets gives every Vienna market's dates + holiday hours in ONE fetch. aachenweihnachtsmarkt.de, spittelberg.at, muenchen.de/christkindlmarkt read clean. wien.orf.at stories fetch clean and are the way to confirm a market actually OPENED (dated, past tense); news.at previews are future tense. **Stale/empty:** weihnachtsmarkt-schoenbrunn.at/en/ shows last season; tivoli.dk opening-hours page is client-side ("Loading"); visitcopenhagen body text lags its calendar; visitbruges publishes only the Winter Glow season, never stall dates (brugge.be/wintergloed 404). **Trap: season end moved earlier** (Bruges Winter Glow Feb 14 -> Jan 31): re-check END dates, not just openings. Organiser pages can mislabel weekdays (Cologne "Di 23.12"): compute them.
- **World events a year out (calendar run, Nov 2026), 28 findings on 8 searches + 41 fetches:** read clean: documenta.de news, labiennale.org `/<discipline>/<year>/information` (dates, closed day, ticket status), liepaja2027.lv/en, evora2027.com/en/news, skulptur-projekte.de/en, glastonburyfestivals.co.uk article pages (the /information/tickets/ index shows headlines only), rydercup.com/attend/ticket-information, oktoberfest.de/en, stpatricksfestival.ie, vividsydney.com + nsw.gov.au event listing, icc-cricket.com media-releases, inside.fifa.com media-releases, world.rugby/news, panamsports.org, city-council news (edinburgh.gov.uk), elperuano.pe (Peru's gazette: ministry statements on Machu Picchu sales), visiticeland.com. **Empty/blocked:** fifa.com tournament articles and ncaa.com (client-side, empty), letour.fr (refused, twice), bie-paris.org 403, expobelgrade2027.org homepage (no dates in text), sanfermin.pamplona.es (connection refused), en.vedur.is aurora page (no season months), rugbyworldcup.com tickets page (status, no dates). Member-federation news (matildas.com.au) carries FIFA dates the FIFA page will not render. Never guess a deep article URL: it returns empty and costs a call.
- **Trap patterns from re-checking an older research file:** (1) an "opens on" date may have PASSED since the file was written (Western Sydney Airport, 25 Oct 2026): rewrite as open, keep only the still-future items. (2) "Ends in <city> on <date>" can be the end of a foreign leg, not the event (Tour de France Femmes UK stages). (3) An "opening" can be a three-day weekend with the headline concert on the middle day (Liepāja). (4) Commercial ticket sites title pages with next year before the authority opens sales (Machu Picchu 2027). (5) A dates announcement can be years old (documenta 16, 2022): confirm it is still live and say how old it is.
- **Trap pattern: the famous event whose operator quit.** Schönbrunn's Christmas market operator (MTS Wien) ended after 2023 while guides still list it. Same family as Vancouver's cancelled Celebration of Light. For any guide-consensus anchor event, check the organiser page still exists for this season. See [[timing-source-lessons]].
