- Official attraction pages (capbridge.com, grousemountain.com, vanmaritime.com) fetch fine. grousemountain.com/general-admission-membership itemises prices and the resident tier; capbridge.com/tickets-and-hours does not itemise prices (only discounts and dates).
- Viator listing pages and shop.capbridge.com return 403; a museum's own admission page can also fail (vancouverartgallery.ca "Socket is closed"). Snippet only, mark access snippet.
- Go City had no Vancouver product page in two searches; do not spend a third.
- Budget counting: count fetches plus searches against the ceiling. 12 went fast (6 searches, 6 fetches). Spend fetches on official pages first; happy hours and tourism-board promos need their own calls, so reserve 3 for them.
- Contract trap: a promo with one source cannot be kind promo in researched mode (needs two independent sites). Record as fact with the end date and say why in notes.
- Money in claims: only official fixed prices may carry a single amount (set fixedPrice true). Aggregator pass prices are omitted from the claim.
- Rail passes (Oct 2026): eurail.com now 301s to interrail.com/en-int (brand merger 24 Sept 2026). interrail.com pass pages do NOT render prices on fetch (country/currency picker), so official pass prices need a different route; do not spend a second fetch there.
- wienerlinien.at/tickets lists ticket types but no prices (prices are in the WienMobil shop). viennacitycard.at/en/vienna-city-card/ fetches cleanly with full official prices.
- strasbourg.eu/web/musees/tarifs-musees-de-strasbourg fetches cleanly (prices, pass, exempt groups) but the fetch summary dropped the first-Sunday rule that the search snippet showed; ask for it explicitly in the prompt.
- Ski runs (Oct 2026): unofficialnetworks.com returns 403 on fetch (snippets only), but its "pass deadlines in the next 10 days" roundups carry Epic/Ikon step dates. highlandsharborsprings.com/season-passes fetches with Boyne pass prices AND the price-step date; boynemountain.com pages hide prices. crystalmountain.com (Michigan) /season-passes gives deadlines and tiers but no prices. nubsnob.com rate-sheet 404s; shop.nubsnob.com gives snippets only.
- Ski Tivoli trap: ikonpass.com "Crystal Mountain" local passes are Washington; search summaries pin them on Michigan.
- Northern Express (northernexpress.com) runs a yearly "Up North staycation deals" stay-and-ski roundup (published late January), good for the pattern, always last season by autumn.
- Resort deadlines usually come only from the resort's own site, so in researched mode they end up kind fact with datedUntil, not promo. derivedFrom cannot point at another agent's file: put break-even arithmetic in notes, citing the costs-xxx ids.
- Session-wide search cap (Oct 2026): WebSearch is capped at ~200 per session SHARED by every agent; late in an 11-agent wave the cap was hit after 1 search. Plan fetch-first from known official URLs and from URLs in earlier agents' JSON; guessed official URLs mostly 404 (alcazarsevilla.org/en/, renfe.com Spain Pass, juntadeandalucia.es/cultura/enclaves/...).
- Andalusia: museosdeandalucia.es fails on a TLS certificate error. alcazabaygibralfaro.malaga.eu/es/... states the Sunday no-charge rule; the /fr/ version omits it. Junta government PDFs (ws040.juntadeandalucia.es) fetch as binary but the saved PDF reads cleanly with Read. malagapass.com renders only template placeholders.
- Pattern: Spanish state/regional museums often give no-charge entry or reduced rates to EU nationals only (residents/EU trap for US visitors), and many close on Mondays. Always check the site's closing day against the event date.
- Airline deal pages (Nov 2026): READ cleanly: southwest.com/deals/ (fare listings only, truncated), news.flyfrontier.com/ (dated index), news.delta.com/, aircanada.com/us/en/aco/home/book/special-offers.html (book-by + travel dates), jetbluevacations.com/trip-ideas/cyber-week-travel-deals, allegiantair.com/preview-cyber-monday, finnair.com/se-en/black-friday. FAIL: jetblue.com/deals 404, flybreeze.com/deals 403, alaskaair.com/content/deals renders title only, easyjet.com (news and BF page) 403, austrian.com 403, etihad.com BF page timeout, virginaustralia.com newsroom/specials ECONNRESET, singaporeair.com guessed URL 404, finnair.com/us-en 404. Do not retry the failures; go to snippets.
- Holiday-sale timing pattern: in early November most airline sale pages still show last year's terms or "coming soon"; U.S. airline sales announce Nov 26 to Dec 1. A run before ~Nov 20 mostly yields "not announced" facts; spend fewer calls on airlines then.
- Holiday-sale refresh (Nov 2026), official URLs that fetch: royalcaribbean.com/cruise-deals/black-friday (renders template placeholders, countdown zeros; no end date), celebritycruises.com/cruise-deals/black-friday, virginvoyages.com/cruise-deals (dated offers; the /black-friday URL shows "expired"), gadventures.com/travel-deals/black-friday-sale/ (full terms), contiki.com/en-us/activity/black-friday, intrepidtravel.com/us/cyber-sale, greyhound.com/black-friday-bus-deals (full terms, stays on last year until relaunch), interrail.com/en-int/terms-conditions/promo-terms-conditions (every promo with dates: best Interrail source), hostelworld.com/deals/black-friday/, hilton.com/en/offers/, amtrak.com/deals-discounts.html, booking.com/deals/index.html (tile only, no terms). 404s: contiki.com/en-us/black-friday, gadventures.com/black-friday/, all.accor.com/promotions-offers/index.en.shtml.
- Blocked: expedia.com/deals 429; ihg.com offers 403; world.hyatt.com offers 403; marriott.com/offers.mi fetches but truncates. TPG's hotel Black Friday page stays on last year until its December update.
- Travel Market Report runs a yearly Black Friday supplier tracker (2026: travelmarketreport.com/cruises/articles/2026-black-friday-travel-promotions): booking windows for cruise/tour/hotel brands; the usable second source for promos. It can disagree with the brand page on the up-to figure: quote the brand.
- Validator: an "up to $950 off" headline counts as a point price. Set fixedPrice true with the brand's official page as a source and say in notes it is a quoted headline, not a fare.
- Trap pattern: coupon sites relabel last year's sale dates with this year; sale pages keep the current year in title/footer over old terms.
- Go City (Nov 2026): gocity.com/en/<city> fetches but prints only "from" prices per pass type (no tiers, no child price). The break-even material is in its article gocity.com/en/<city>/things-to-do/should-i-buy-the-go-city-<city>-pass-or-individual-tickets: a dated worked example with gate prices per attraction. It is the seller's best case; cross-check one gate price against the attraction's own page.
- iventurecard.com returns 403 on fetch. A 403 is not proof the product is still sold; record brand status as unconfirmed.
- A URL slug taken from a search result can still 404 (sydneyexpert.com/sydney-attraction-passes/). Search results listing a "?p=" form alongside a slug are a hint the slug moved.
- Search summaries often say "one aggregator lists..." without naming the page. Those prices cannot be cited; leave them out and gap the price.
- One search naming four museums in four cities returned almost nothing usable. Fetch each museum's own visit page (ngv.vic.gov.au/plan-your-visit/ fetches cleanly) or gap the city.
- Event ticket pages (rugbyworldcup.com/2027/en/tickets) print sale status but no prices or discount categories; prices sit in the purchase flow. Starting prices come from the launch press release.
- Concession searches return university student pages, not the transport agency. Useful for the overseas-student exclusion; seniors need the agency's own page.
- Multi-city runs: 12 calls cover roughly 3-4 cities. Pick the cities where a pass or no-charge day actually changes the plan, and gap the rest honestly.
