Learned on the European Christmas Markets run (2026-10-06, 12-call budget).

**Fetch worked (read):**
- aeroroutes.com schedule-filing posts: clean, dated, carrier + weekly frequency + date ranges. Best source for seasonal cuts. Search "aeroroutes <carrier> NW26 <city>".
- ratepunk.com `/flights-from-to/<ORIG>/<DEST>/...`: month-by-month medians, typical range, refresh date. Aggregator, but readable; use as one of two fare sources.

**Snippet-only / weak:**
- Airline route pages (airlineinformation, flightsfrom, directflights, idealo, roame) mostly reflect SUMMER schedules; never take them as winter confirmation.
- Search summaries for "Detroit nonstop Europe" hallucinated that Delta has no winter DTW-Europe service. Trust only named-route snippets.
- Kayak route pages: snippet numbers, direction of route often unclear.
- Google's annual holiday-travel blog (blog.google/products/search/holiday-travel-trends-YYYY) carries the days-before-departure finding; widely re-reported (Time Out, Travel Noire), so two sources come easily.

**Traps that recur:**
- Article year: infer from schedule-season dates (IATA winter = last Sunday Oct; summer = last Sunday Mar). A "stops Oct 25, resumes Mar 29" article was 2025-26, not 2026-27.
- MSP winter service changes year to year: KLM was summer-only in 2024-25 and returned to winter flying for NW26 (with a Jan-Feb pause); Icelandair paused Jan-Feb 2025 but not 2026; Delta MSP-DUB went year-round in NW26 after Aer Lingus left (Oct 2026). Never carry a "loses X in winter" line forward; read the current NW filing and FlightMapper periods.
- Budget tip: 3 searches on hub nonstops, 1 aeroroutes fetch, 2-3 on fares/lead time, 1 basic economy, 1 airport-name traps. Leaves smaller origins (MKE/GRR/IND/CLE/CMH) uncovered at 12 calls; ask for more or search them first if the brief stresses them.
- Origin-neutral event runs (eclipse-2027-spain, 2026-10-12): Wikipedia airport pages (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/<Airport>) fetch cleanly and give distance to centre, rail station, Aena passenger counts and a full airlines/destinations table in one call; pair each with a second host (tourspain.es B2B connectivity page is an official, readable 2026 route round-up for Spain). Gibraltar Airport schedule only came via snippets.
- Event demand: Euronews (es.euronews.com) carries airline booking-surge statements; they include promo lead-in fares that are point prices, leave them out.
- FareCompare route pages give "cheapest month" labels from ordinary years; useful only as a trap for event weeks, never as a fare band.
- Read the earlier agents' JSON first: path/edge-of-path facts and arrival-day traffic were already sourced; cite their read sources with a "not re-read here" note rather than spending calls.
- Australia event run (rugby-world-cup-2027, 2026-10-12): Wikipedia pages for Sydney, Perth and Townsville airports fetched cleanly with terminal layouts (Perth's Qantas T3/T4 vs T1/T2 split is a recurring trap). Two Wikipedia pages are ONE host for the validator's two-source rule; pair each route claim with aeroroutes, aerocorner, airportroutes or an airline release. Small airports (Newcastle NTL) came only via snippets.
- Fare searches phrased "London to Sydney" returned only AUD, Sydney-origin pages; for a GBP band search a UK-domain site (skyscanner.net, kayak.co.uk) by name.
- Schedules load about 11 months out (EWN quoting a sports-travel agent, Oct 2026). For events 12+ months away, say fares are not observable yet and give ordinary-year baselines labelled as such.
- Validator scans notes too: write money as "AUD 1,151 to 2,171", never "AUD 1,151 (cheapest) to ..." or a lone figure.
- Lead-time evidence disagrees (Google 32-73 days vs blogs saying 3-6 months); report both, see [[fare-intelligence-rule]].

Ireland / Ryder Cup run (ryder-cup-2027, 2026-10-16, 25 calls counting fetches):
- Read cleanly: cbp.gov preclearance page (all locations + hours), snnairportgroup.ie news releases (official Shannon routes, seats), dublinairport.com /flight-information/travelling-to-usa/usa-preclearance, wxyz.com route news, aeroroutes Aer Lingus filings. travelextra.ie slug URLs time out; the `?p=<id>` form fetched fine.
- 404s: delta.com basic-economy fare-class page; dublinairport.com /at-the-airport/us-preclearance. Delta's agency page pro.delta.com came only as a snippet.
- Off-season trap: in October, KAYAK says "no direct flights" for seasonal routes (DTW-DUB runs May-Oct). Never read an off-season aggregator as "no nonstop".
- RatePunk mislabels connecting carriers (Turkish, JetBlue) as "direct" on DTW-DUB; use its monthly medians, not its carrier list.
- Sales-window arithmetic: Delta/AA 331 days, United ~337 (some say 330); the RETURN date is the binding one for a round trip.
- Internal framework logistics lines go stale (dublin.js said "DTW has no nonstop"; Delta launched it in 2025). Flag them to founders when found.
- Airport capacity caps (Dublin) can block new routes for event years; search "<airport> passenger cap" for capped hubs.

Midwest fare-bands run (midwest-fares, 2026-11-22, DTW part, 10 searches + ~70 fetches):
- **Kayak `kayak.com/flight-routes/<City>-<IATA>/<City>-<IATA>` pages fetch cleanly** and carry everything the fares brief wants: typical range, 12-month average, cheapest/dearest month medians, low/high season label, connection-airport shares, one-stop average totals, and a "book N weeks ahead saves X%" line. Best single fare source found. Its FAQ "only Delta operates" and "no direct flights" lines contradict the body; read the body.
- **RatePunk URL uses the CITY code for Detroit: `/flights-from-to/DTT/<IATA>/cheap-flights-from-detroit-to-<city>`** (DTW 404s). Has no Sydney page. Its "direct flights" airline list is garbage (Frontier, JetBlue as DTW-DUB nonstops) and its "typical journey" times are cheapest-fare artefacts (45h, 3 stops). Use medians only.
- Cheapflights and Momondo are Kayak-owned: not an independent second host for the validator's purposes; say so in notes.
- **Blocked/unusable for fares:** expedia.com (429 on every URL incl. newsroom; Orbitz/Travelocity too), Google Flights static pages `google.com/travel/flights/flights-from-X-to-Y.html` (verification interstitial), Skyscanner (JS shell), FareCompare and flightsfrom.com (403), trip.com and aviasales route pages (404). delta.com `flights-from-X-to-Y` pages 404.
- **pro.delta.com agency Basic Economy page reads cleanly** (consumer page 404): the $199 long-haul cancellation charge, 30-day seat window, no-miles rule.
- aeroroutes filings list CHANGES only; a route absent from an NS27 filing is not absent from the schedule. Delta News Hub `/tags/<city>` pages hold images, not articles.
- Wikipedia airport pages need a second fetch with `offset: 100000` to reach Ground transport. Official transit pages that read: trenitalia.com Leonardo Express, pid.cz/en/airport (CZK fares), airportlink.com.au/trip-information/price (gate pass). dublinairport.com bus page names no operators or fares; prg.aero/en/public-transport 404.
- Google's booking-window blog is now the 2025 edition (`holiday-travel-trends-2025`): Europe 48+ days, international 49+. Going's guide (2 to 8 months) has no methodology. Expedia 2026 editions disagree by market (US 31 to 45 days, UK 15 to 30).
- Spanning two aggregators' typical ranges (Kayak low to RatePunk high) is the honest "range" for a card; state in notes that low/high are published bounds, not arithmetic, so the validator does not demand derivedFrom.

Midwest fares run (midwest-fares, 2026-11-22, 12 searches + ~60 fetches, ORD part):
- **KAYAK route pages fetch cleanly and are the best historical-fare source found:** `kayak.com/flight-routes/Chicago-O-Hare-Intl-ORD/<City>-<IATA>` (e.g. `Rome-Fiumicino-FCO`, `Sydney-Kingsford-Smith-SYD`). Each gives a 12-month typical round-trip range, cheapest and two dearest month medians, nonstop carriers with weekly counts, nonstop duration, one-stop hub shares, and a "cheapest N weeks before departure" line with a last-updated date. Origin-specific (ORD, not CHI). Its nonstop-duration line is garbled on no-nonstop routes; ignore it there.
- **RatePunk URL format:** `/flights-from-to/CHI/<DEST>/cheap-flights-from-chicago-to-<city>` (city code CHI, not ORD; `/ORD/DUB` 404s). Full 12-month median table plus a booking-window table, but it blends ORD/MDW/RFD and its Prague and Sydney pages carry medians inconsistent with their own ranges. Use as the second host, never alone; say so in notes.
- **Fare bands that pass the validator and stay honest:** value = the span of published medians for the season's months across both aggregators; the claim names which source sits at each end. Where only one source publishes medians (shoulder months), cite the other's whole-year range as the check and drop confidence to low. Never write a lone figure in notes either (a "$450 apart" remark had to be rewritten).
- **Blocked this run:** united.com (timeout x3), lufthansa.com, aa.com, ita-airways.com (403), aerlingus.com (CAPTCHA), expedia.com/newsroom (403/429), businesswire (429), skyscanner (JS shell), farecompare (403), aena.es/munich-airport.com/prg.aero/mvv transport pages (404 at guessed URLs), wsiairport.com.au (JS-only), infrastructure.gov.au (timeout). Readable: Wikipedia airport pages (destination tables carry Seasonal marks; split long pages with offset 100000), aeroroutes posts (some short posts return only a title), united.mediaroom.com, airnewzealand.com route pages, cbp.gov, going.com guides, money.com, click2houston, dublinexpress.ie, trenitalia.com, pid.cz/en/airport, info.flightmapper.net flight pages (schedule periods by date range: good for a carrier's seasonality).
- **Expedia Air Hacks** primary page blocks; press reports disagree with the snippet of the primary (31-45 days vs 8-15 days for international). Record both.
- **GRR part lessons (same run):** RatePunk IS origin-specific for small airports: `/flights-from-to/GRR/<DEST>/cheap-flights-from-grand-rapids-to-<city>` worked for DUB, BCN, PRG, MUC but 404'd for FCO and SYD (both URL forms); its GRR journey-time and stopover figures are artefacts (65h 49m to Dublin). KAYAK small-airport pages use `Grand-Rapids-GRR` for Europe but `Grand-Rapids-Gerald-R-Ford-Intl-GRR` for domestic hubs; they print one-stop hub shares, average totals and mean connection times (7h+ at ORD from GRR), but often no booking-window line. cheapflights.com route pages fetch and print monthly stats but are a KAYAK brand: same data family, not a second source. flyford.org is now grr.org (nonstop table is JS, unreadable; news releases read fine). Readable: rome2rio `/s/<City>-<ST>-USA/<Airport-Name>-<IATA>` (drive distance, time, transit), flychicago.com and metroairport.com parking rate pages (operator rates, usable as ranges), info.flightmapper.net QF_8 and LH_435. Blocked: travelmath and distance-cities (403), amtrak.com route pages (404; use the Wikipedia train article), qantas.com (timeout), delta.com basic-economy and route pages (404), internationalairportreview (410).
- **Watchdog trap:** a batch of 10+ WebFetch calls in one turn stalled the harness twice (600 s no progress) and the file never landed. Write the run file FIRST from what is held, then fetch at most three pages per step and rewrite between steps.
- **MSP part (same run):** KAYAK slug is `Minneapolis-St-Paul-MSP`; RatePunk uses `/MSP/<DEST>/cheap-flights-from-minneapolis-to-<city>` (MSP is its own city code, no blending). RatePunk's MSP pages print bogus "direct flight" carrier lists on no-nonstop routes and 2- or 3-stop "typical" ranges above the round-trip regular range: use only the regular range and medians. mspairport.com: `/node/1251` is the official airline-by-terminal list, `/node/1056` the between-terminals page; `/airport/airlines`, `/airport/terminals` and the light-rail page 404, and metrotransit.org Blue Line URLs 404 too. `info.flightmapper.net/flight/<Carrier>_<FltNo>` gives schedule periods by date range and is the cleanest way to see a route's season gaps (DL298, FI656). Delta NW/NS intercontinental filings on aeroroutes (`2605xx-dlnw26inc`, `2601xx-dlns26inc`) list MSP lines; news.delta.com and theflightclub.it block or fail. WebFetch can fail on a classifier outage for several calls in a row: write the file before the last fetch wave.
- **2027 seasonal dates were unfindable in November 2026:** no NS27 aeroroutes filing carries Chicago-Europe lines for United or American (they filed only new routes). Record the prior-year season as a pattern, labelled, and a could-not-look gap.
