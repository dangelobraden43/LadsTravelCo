description: Durable source lessons for lodging/neighbourhood research - which pages give distances, why snippets cannot give price ranges, STR article dating traps

  type: reference
---

- **Resort "off-site lodging" pages are the best single source for slopeside-vs-town layout.** They list partner lodging with the resort's own distance figures (Lutsen's did; read in one fetch). Fetch these before searching.
- **Aggregator snippets (Expedia, Hotels.com, Travelocity) only show single "from" prices.** They cannot become a `range` and the validator rejects point prices. Budget calls for resort lodging pages or a tourism bureau rate page if price tiers are required; otherwise record `lodging-price-ranges` as a gap early rather than burning calls.
- **Local-press STR articles are often years old.** A search for "2026 rules" returned a 2018 Northern Express piece and a 2020 Traverse Ticker piece. Always state the article date and whether final adoption was confirmed; the city code itself is the source to read next time.
- **Tourism-bureau pages lag renames** (Petoskey bureau still said Boyne Highlands in 2026). Cross-check names against lads-provenance before using them.
- **Ontario towns publish STR licensing by-laws on municipal sites** (thebluemountains.ca, collingwood.ca); pair with local press (collingwoodtoday.ca) for the two-host rule.
- **Spain: INE's API gives hotel ADR for every tourist point in ONE fetch.** `https://servicios.ine.es/wstempus/js/ES/DATOS_TABLA/46298?nult=2` returns the last two months per town (government source, read). Each town's two months make an honest range. It is an all-category average (no tiers) and a normal-season floor, never an event-week price. Small towns (Conil, Vejer) are not tourist points. Other countries' statistics offices may have the same shape; look for it before searching aggregators.
- **Small-town "where to stay" searches (Tarifa) return one booking-site guide cloned across 10 languages.** Near-zero value; spend the call on the town's tourist office page instead.
- **Event "how fast beds fill" evidence = CoStar/STR press releases** (they publish match-night occupancy and ADR % change per host city for big events). costar.com returns 403 on fetch; str.com and hospitalitynet.org re-posts show in snippets. Next time fetch the hospitalitynet re-post to upgrade to `read`. Their ADR figures are single citywide averages, so they cannot become a tier range. Percent changes can go in claims.
- **Comparable events in a winter low season overstate % jumps** for a spring/summer event; say so as a trap.
- **Regional-city bed shortages show up in local press plus the regional tourism body's news archive** (e.g. a tourism body brokering rate ceilings and minimum-stay warnings for a concert). Search "<city> accommodation booked out <past event>".
- **"Hotels near <stadium>" searches return only aggregator listings.** The city tourism board's own stadium page (pre/post-game guide) lists nearby streets with walk times; search for that page by name instead.
- **Australian STR rules:** NSW = Planning Portal STRA register + 180-night Greater Sydney cap; WA = mandatory register (all sources are *.wa.gov.au, one publisher, so add a press source if it must be a `requirement`); Victoria = SRO short stay levy. Queensland/SA not yet checked.
- **Ireland:** STR register rules = enterprise.gov.ie short-term-letting page (government, read; undated, blogs disagree on start date). limerickleader.ie returns 403; travelextra.ie fetch timed out; limerickpost.ie, clarechampion.ie, irishexaminer.com and limerick.ie fetch fine. Council news pages (limerick.ie/node/...) restate organiser prices and make the second source.
- **Ireland small towns (Clare, Oct 2026):** clare.fm fetches fine and carries county bed-stock/STR stories; Wikivoyage town pages give layout, station distance and pub-closing hours in one read fetch; bustimes.org route pages (e.g. 350) give stop order + full timetable (NTA data) in one fetch and pair with a transportforireland.ie news page for the two-host bar. Failte Ireland hotel-survey PDFs 404 at old URLs and its assets.failteireland.ie PowerPoint PDFs do not extract; no county ADR series was found, so record tier prices as a gap on call one.
- **Check the destination's own festival calendar for the event week** (Lisdoonvarna Matchmaking Festival fills north Clare all September). Discover Ireland event pages read cleanly.
- **Single-venue event official lodging:** the organiser's travel-services FAQ page carries the real terms (minimum nights, deposit, cancellation coupling, occupancy per room); its listings pages render empty to WebFetch (JS). The event's own *transport* page ranks bases (which towns are rail feeds to the shuttle) better than any "where to stay" search.
- **City tier ranges without a stats office: HotelsCombined `/Place/<City>.htm` reads in one fetch** with year-round 3/4/5-star averages, dearest/cheapest month and day of week (GBP on .co.uk). One site only; pair with Skyscanner's county page (snippet) and say it is weak. Proves "September/Saturday is the dearest night" cheaply.
- **Dublin neighbourhood sources that fetch:** timeout.com/dublin/hotels/where-to-stay-in-dublin (dated) and theirishroadtrip.com/dublin-neighborhoods (13 areas, transit notes). lonelyplanet.com articles return nav only; hostelz.com and businesspost.ie return 403; travelextra.ie timed out twice (snippet only). Older "areas to avoid" press (thejournal.ie) is 2023; no current Garda street data found.
- **Validator scans titles/notes for single amounts:** strip "EUR 55,000"-style figures out of press headlines in source titles and notes, or fold them into a range.
- **Ireland, fetch behaviour (Oct 17 2026):** discoverireland.ie venue/listing pages (`/kerry/<venue-slug>`) read fine and are official (Failte Ireland), good for a town's after-dark character; `discoverireland.ie/kerry/killarney` is a 404. ireland.com magazine guides read fine and show an "updated" date. lonelyplanet.com articles render nav only (content truncated) - do not fetch. hri.ie returns 403; air.ie racecourse pages read fine. roseoftralee.ie "where to stay" is a 2023 hotel list (gives edge-of-town distances, nothing else). balls.ie reads fine.
- **Ireland STR threshold:** the planning-compliance line is towns over 20,000 (CSO Census 2022). Tralee 26,079 is over it; Killarney, Listowel, Dingle are under. CSO Profile 1 population-distribution page carries the town figures.
- **The WebSearch pool is shared by every agent in the session (200 cap).** In a multi-agent run it can run out mid-run (Oct 17: at call 15). Spend searches early, keep a list of known-good URLs to fetch, and fetch rather than search where a URL is guessable.
- **This agent may have no Bash tool.** If `node tools/research/validate.mjs` cannot be run, read validate.mjs and check by hand: money regex (any currency figure not part of a range, notes included), distinct hosts for actionable kinds, dates <= today, derivedFrom ids exist.
- **limerickleader.ie 403 workaround:** adarevillage.com republishes Limerick Leader Ryder Cup pieces in full and fetches fine. balls.ie and clare.fm also fetch fine.
- **Council PDFs (limerick.ie forward-planning updates) fetch as binary** and the Read tool cannot render PDFs here (no pdftoppm). Their search snippet is all you get; mark `snippet`.
- **Student summer lets end before September events.** Irish/UK universities start autumn semester in early September (UL 2027: 6 Sept) and summer lets close in late August, even on a page titled for the event. Check the academic calendar before offering campus beds for any Sept-Dec event.
- **Check that a hostel exists before pricing a hostel tier.** Some mid-size cities have none; local planning-application press is the quickest evidence.
- **Validator money scan only fires on numbers with a currency marker** (EUR, euro, the euro sign). Write arithmetic in notes as bare numbers ("7,564 to 8,422 divided by 5").
- **WebFetch can be refused permission for a whole run (Galway, Oct 17 2026).** Test one fetch first; if refused, plan snippet-only: every finding drops to low/medium and the read-upgrades go in a `read-access` gap naming the URLs to fetch next time.
- **Apartment-for-a-group tier without fetching:** AirDNA (`airdna.co/vacation-rental-data/app/ie/default/<city>/revenue`) and AirROI (`airroi.com/airbnb-data/<country>/<county>/<city>`) snippets each give a trailing-12-month ADR; two hosts make a researched `range`. All listing types, so say entire homes sit at or above the top. momondo.<cc>/hotels/<city> snippets give hotel averages by star class (same Kayak group as HotelsCombined, so not independent of it; pair with press ADR instead).
- **Ireland 2026 STR law moved twice:** the 21-night definition (Residential Tenancies (Misc Provisions) Act 2026 s.30) and an April 2026 SI on the principal-private-residence exemption. irishstatutebook.ie and revisedacts.lawreform.ie snippets show enactment and commencement notes. galwaybayfm.ie carries Galway STR enforcement and Eyre Square night-time stories (dates not shown in snippets).
- 12 calls covers ~5 bases plus STR rules with no prices. Price tiers need their own budget. See [[stay-run-strategy]].
