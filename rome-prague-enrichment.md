# ROME (+ Naples / Pompeii / Amalfi)

## Framework-level

**The booking reality (practical — this is the framework's most valuable tip block):**
- Rome's big three now run strict timed entry and sell out: Colosseum, Vatican Museums, Galleria Borghese. *(2026 guide consensus)*
- **Colosseum:** tickets release ~30 days ahead on the official park site (ticketing.colosseo.it / parcocolosseo.it — beware lookalike domains that are resellers). Standard tickets: book 2–3 weeks out spring/fall, 4–6 weeks summer. Underground/arena access: ~2 months out or via a licensed tour operator's allocation. One ticket covers Colosseum + Roman Forum + Palatine Hill (one entry each, same visit).
- **Vatican:** morning slots sell out 2–3 weeks ahead in peak. Sequence tip from the guides: lock the Vatican slot FIRST, build the Colosseum around it.
- **Getting around (FOUNDER):** "Buy a metro card and use the public transport. The center connects fast, and driving in Rome is a mistake you only make once." *(expanded from Brady's metro-card line; consensus emphatically agrees: don't drive, Metro B stops directly at 'Colosseo')*

**theTrap (framework-level):** The "skip the line" sellers around the Colosseum — including the gladiator-costume guys — are a documented scam lane: fake QR codes at worst, 4x-price real tickets at best. Nothing sold on the plaza beats booking the official site a month out. *(2026 scam guides)*

## Spots

### The Colosseum
- **FOUNDER draft:** "My favorite monument anywhere. Pictures don't prepare you for standing inside it. Give it real time instead of rushing through for the photo."
- **CONSENSUS:** Universal must-see; the guided-tour take is strong here — the Forum and Palatine ruins next door are fascinating but nearly unreadable without context, and they're included in your ticket anyway.
- **TIPS:** Book the release window (above). Morning entry beats afternoon heat. Sneakers — stairs and ancient paving throughout. No dress code (unlike the Vatican).

### Vatican City (Basilica + Museums)
- **FOUNDER draft:** "Truly breathtaking. Do both the Basilica and the museums, and don't treat it as a quick stop. It earns a half day minimum."
- **CONSENSUS:** The museums (with the Sistine Chapel) are the timed-entry booking; St. Peter's Basilica is free entry but shares the airport-style security line with everyone.
- **TIPS (the little things):** Dress code enforced with no exceptions — knees AND shoulders covered, men and women; pack a scarf in summer. Arrive 20 minutes before your museum slot for security. There are cafeterias inside the museums — you don't need to exit for lunch.

### Pompeii (day trip)
- **FOUNDER draft:** "Truly one of a kind. Walking streets that stopped in 79 AD does something to you. Worth building a whole day around."
- **CONSENSUS:** Timed entry with a 20,000/day cap mid-March to mid-October; July–August sell out entirely. Official tickets moved to Vivaticket in March 2026 [CC verify current vendor at ingestion].
- **TIPS:** From Naples ~35 min on the Circumvesuviana — **get off at "Pompei Scavi – Villa dei Misteri," NOT Pompei town** (the classic first-timer mistake). Porta Marina is the main entrance by the station. Sturdy shoes (hard basalt paving), sun protection (almost no shade), screenshot your QR before you lose signal, ID must match the ticket.

### Amalfi Coast (day trips)
- **FOUNDER draft:** "Highly recommend the day trips down the coast. It's a long day from Rome, but the coastline is the point of the whole region."
- **CONSENSUS + honesty note:** Amalfi day trips run via Naples — from Rome it's a long day (the existing affiliate disambiguation rule already records this: Amalfi tours depart Naples, not Rome). Frame honestly: best done from Naples/Sorrento; doable from Rome as a full-commitment day.

### YellowSquare Hostel (stay)
- **FOUNDER draft:** "We stayed at YellowSquare and I'd recommend it to any younger crowd, or grab a private room if dorms aren't your thing. Bar discounts, free food events, and walking tours run out of the lobby. It made the trip cheaper and better at the same time."
- **forWho:** "Younger crews and budget trips that still want a social base."

### Trastevere (district)
- **FOUNDER draft:** "Cross the river for the cafés and bars. Trastevere at night is the Rome you pictured before you came."
- **CONSENSUS:** The consensus evening district; cobblestones, trattorias, aperitivo. Crowded on weekend nights — early evening is the calmer window.

# PRAGUE + DRESDEN

## Framework-level

**Budget honesty (FOUNDER):** "Prague is the best budget trip in Europe we've taken. Views, activities, and world-class pilsner, all at prices that feel like a mistake. That's the pitch and it's true."
- **CONSENSUS (numbers that back it):** Pub beer runs CZK 40–80 (~$2–3.50); a neighborhood-pub goulash ~CZK 170 vs CZK 350–450 on Old Town Square for worse quality. *(2026 value guides)*

**theTrap (framework-level — the money block; this is flagship Challenge content):**
- **Currency:** Czechia uses the koruna (CZK), not the euro. Always pay in CZK — if a card machine offers your home currency, decline it (dynamic currency conversion is a markup, not a convenience).
- **ATMs:** Never use the bright yellow/blue Euronet-style standalone ATMs clustered at tourist sites — predatory rates and fees. Use ATMs attached to real Czech banks (Česká spořitelna, ČSOB, Komerční banka), two blocks off the squares.
- **Exchange booths:** "0% commission" boards near Old Town and Wenceslas Square quote rates 10–30% under market. If you must exchange, ask exactly how many koruna you'll receive BEFORE handing over cash — Czech law gives you 3 hours to cancel with a receipt, and they hope you don't know that.
- **Taxis:** Don't hail from the street in tourist zones. Bolt / Uber / Liftago show the price up front.
- **Menus:** A sidewalk menu in five languages with no prices is the warning sign. Walk on.

**Practical:** Tipping ~10% in restaurants (check the bill — some tourist-area places add service now). Keep 500–1,000 CZK cash — traditional pubs (hospody) and market stalls are often cash-preferred.

## Spots

### Charles Bridge (+ the stay)
- **FOUNDER draft:** "We stayed near Charles Bridge and it was the right call. You cross it constantly, and having it as your default walk home is the kind of thing you remember."
- **CONSENSUS:** The most-photographed spot in the city; sunrise is the move for a near-empty bridge; pickpockets work the daytime crowds — normal awareness applies.

### Letná Beer Garden
- **FOUNDER draft:** "The lookout beer garden above the river. Cheap beer, the whole city below you, and nobody rushing you. Our favorite kind of place."
- **CONSENSUS (validating):** Independently called arguably Prague's best sunset spot, with ~CZK 50 beers — a free-view rival to any paid tower. Seasonal: the garden runs in the warm months [CC verify current season dates].
- **TIP:** Free experience — no booking link ever. Pair with Prague Castle (below) for one hillside day.

### Prague Castle
- **FOUNDER draft:** "Do the castle. The complex is bigger than you expect, and the views over the rooftops are the postcard."
- **CONSENSUS:** The world's largest ancient castle complex by area; grounds are free to walk, interiors are the ticket; mornings beat the tour-group waves.

### Strahov Monastery Brewery
- **FOUNDER draft:** "Our favorite restaurant of the whole trip. A brewery inside a working monastery near the castle. Order the dark lager and whatever's roasting."
- **CONSENSUS:** Monastic brewing site with centuries of history, minutes from the castle — the consensus pick for exactly the meal Brady describes.

### Pilsen + Pilsner Urquell Tour (day trip)
- **FOUNDER draft:** "Highly recommend the day trip to Pilsen and the Pilsner Urquell tour. Seeing where pilsner was invented, then drinking it unfiltered in the cellars, is the best beer experience we've had."
- **CONSENSUS:** The brewery tour's cellar pour (unfiltered, unpasteurized from oak) is the consistently-cited highlight. Book the English tour time ahead [CC verify current booking mechanics]. ~1 hour from Prague by train/bus.
- **Note:** This spot already carries an approved Viator link — endorsement gradient: gold, founders did this.

### Dog Bar / Duplex (nightlife)
- **FOUNDER draft:** "For the younger party crowd, Dog Bar and Duplex are the Prague nights out. Go in knowing what they are and you'll have a great time."
- **forWho:** "The crew night out. Not the quiet-pint evening."

### Dresden (1–2 day trip)
- **FOUNDER draft:** "Highly recommend one or two days in Dresden. The rebuilt old town is one of the most powerful history lessons in Europe, and the architecture is unreal in person."
- **CONSENSUS:** A couple of hours from Prague by direct train [CC verify current schedule]; the Frauenkirche and Zwinger anchor the rebuilt Altstadt.
- **Note:** Dresden already carries a 9/10 rating and an approved Viator day-trip link in prague.js — preserve both.
